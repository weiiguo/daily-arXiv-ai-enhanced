### [1] [Counterexamples to Charpin's Conjecture on BCH codes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28741)
*Run Zheng,Yaoran Yang,Yutong Zhang,Maosheng Xiong*

Main category: cs.IT

TL;DR: 本文构造了一个无限族本原窄感BCH码，其最小距离严格超过Bose距离。通过利用广义Reed-Muller码的重量可除性，作者证明当设计距离为δ时，Bose距离等于δ而最小距离至少为δ+q^s（q=2时取等号）。特别地，通过设定s=t-1，得到一个二元BCH码子族，其最小距离与Bose距离之间的差距至少以码长的立方根增长，严格超过4（对所有m≥13），从而否定了Charpin猜想。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 确定BCH码的最小距离是一个长期存在的困难问题。Bose距离给出了最小距离的下界，但实际最小距离往往更大。此前关于最小距离是否严格超过Bose距离的问题存在争议，特别是Charpin猜想认为在某些条件下差距不会太大。本文旨在构造反例，证明存在一类BCH码，其最小距离与Bose距离的差距可以任意大（以码长立方根级别增长），从而推翻Charpin猜想。

Method: 本文通过以下方法实现：(1) 构造参数化的BCH码族，设q为素数幂，m≥10且m≠12，定义u=⌊m/4⌋, t=⌊(m-1)/3⌋，对u≤s<t的整数s定义设计距离δ=q^m−q^{m−1}−q^{m−1−u}−q^s−1；(2) 利用广义Reed-Muller码的重量可除性（weight divisibility）性质来分析BCH码的最小距离；(3) 通过选择合适的参数s=t−1来最大化最小距离与Bose距离之间的差距。

Result: 主要结果：(1) 证明所构造的每个BCH码的Bose距离为δ，最小距离至少为δ+q^s；(2) 当q=2时等号成立，即最小距离恰好等于δ+q^s；(3) 对二元子族（s=t−1），最小距离与Bose距离的差距至少以码长的立方根增长，对所有m≥13严格超过4；(4) 这些结果否定了Charpin猜想。

Conclusion: 本文成功构造了一个无限族BCH码，其最小距离显著超过Bose距离，差距可以达到码长的立方根量级，这直接推翻了Charpin猜想。这一结果深化了我们对BCH码最小距离行为的理解，表明在某些参数范围内，Bose距离下界远非紧的。所采用的广义Reed-Muller码重量可除性方法也为此类问题提供了新的分析工具。

Abstract: Determining the exact minimum distance of BCH codes is a longstanding and challenging problem. In this paper, we construct an infinite family of primitive narrow-sense BCH codes whose minimum distance strictly exceeds their Bose distance. Let $q$ be a prime power, let $m$ be an integer with $m \geq 10$ and $m \neq 12$, and set $u = \lfloor m/4 \rfloor$ and $t = \lfloor (m-1)/3 \rfloor$. For each integer $s$ with $u \leq s < t$, we define$$δ= q^m - q^{m-1} - q^{m-1-u} - q^s - 1.$$We prove that the primitive narrow-sense BCH code with designed distance $δ$ has Bose distance $δ$ and a minimum distance of at least $δ+ q^s$, with equality holding for $q = 2$. Furthermore, by setting $s = t - 1$, we derive a subfamily of binary BCH codes in which the gap between the minimum distance and the Bose distance grows at least as the cube root of the code length, strictly exceeding $4$ for all $m \geq 13$. This disproves Charpin's conjecture. We identify these BCH codes by exploiting the weight divisibility properties of generalized Reed--Muller codes.

### [4] [The Entropic Sum-Product Phenomenon](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.29042)
*Rupert Li*

Main category: cs.IT

TL;DR: 本文证明了离散随机变量的香农熵的和积现象：max{H(X+X'), H(XX')} ≥ (8/7)H(X) - O(log H(X))，回答了Goh提出的问题（系数严格大于1即可），并给出了8/7的系数。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: Goh提出了熵版本的和积现象问题，即能否找到严格大于1的系数。之前Gavalakis等人的工作证明了一个较弱的形式，但由于最小熵可能远小于香农熵的例子，无法获得严格大于1的系数。本文旨在克服这一障碍，建立强于1的系数。

Method: 本文采用两种关键技术：（1）将X的分布分割成均匀块（uniformization），代价是O(log H(X))的熵损失，从而规避最小熵远小于香农熵的问题，首先获得10/9的系数；（2）借鉴Solymosi在组合和积现象中通过限制乘法能量获得4/3系数的工作，将其适配到熵设置，再通过均匀化技术将系数提升至8/7。

Result: 证明了max{H(X+X'), H(XX')} ≥ (8/7)H(X) - O(log H(X))，系数为8/7，优于初始的10/9。作者之前的例子表明系数不能超过4/3。

Conclusion: 本文建立了熵版本和积现象的实质性进展，将系数从1提升到8/7，同时8/7与已知上界4/3之间仍有差距，为后续研究留下空间。

Abstract: Let $X,X'$ be independent and identically distributed discrete real-valued random variables of finite Shannon entropy, and write $H(X)$ for the Shannon entropy of $X$. We prove that \[
  \max\{H(X+X'),\,H(XX')\} \ge \frac87 H(X)-O(\log H(X)). \] This is the entropic analog of the celebrated sum-product phenomenon, and answers a question of Goh, which simply asked for a coefficient strictly larger than 1. An example by the author, Gavalakis, and Kontoyiannis showed the coefficient cannot exceed $\frac43$. Previous work by Gavalakis, Goh, and Kontoyiannis was able to prove a result of a weaker form, which could not translate to a coefficient strictly larger than 1 because of examples where the min-entropy is significantly smaller than the Shannon entropy. By splitting the distribution of $X$ into uniform pieces, which costs $O(\log H(X))$ entropy, we obviate this issue, establishing a coefficient of $\frac{10}{9}$. We augment this to $\frac87$ by adapting the work of Solymosi, which established the combinatorial sum-product phenomenon with coefficient $\frac43$ by bounding the multiplicative energy, to the entropy setting, again via a uniformization technique.
